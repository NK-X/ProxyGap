# ProxyGap Formal v1: Retrospective Analysis Record

**Record date:** 10 August 2026  
**Status:** completed exploratory study; retrospective analysis only  
**Not a preregistration:** condition and intervention selection preceded this record

## 1. Scope

Formal v1 is retained as an exploratory Ant-v5/PPO diagnosis of control-cost misspecification. It does not test the bounded effort/orientation intervention specified in Proposal_G6. The historical `shaped` condition is reported as exploratory forward-reward reweighting.

The study does not establish formal reward hacking because no independently justified scalar true reward was defined. The intended terminology is `proxy–diagnostic divergence`, `common-rescore difference` and `multi-objective trade-off`.

## 3. Discovery and replication structure

- `20260808` is the discovery training seed used in the coefficient sweep and subsequent condition selection.
- `20260809` and `20260810` are fresh optimiser seeds for the three core conditions.
- The ten evaluation reset seeds are paired across conditions but are reused between discovery and replication stages.
- `0.25` and `0.125` remain single-seed exploratory conditions and cannot identify a coefficient threshold or dose-response curve.

Discovery, fresh-seed replication and the combined three-seed summary must be shown separately before any combined mean is interpreted.

## 4. Corrected estimands

### 4.1 Condition-specific objective

`condition_objective_return` is the numerical reward actually optimised in each condition. It is retained to describe training incentives, but raw values are not interpreted as a fixed-proxy improvement across different control-cost weights.

### 4.2 Common rescore

Every evaluation episode is counterfactually rescored under `ctrl_cost_weight=0.5`:

```text
common_rescored_return
  = reward_forward_sum
  + reward_survive_sum
  + reward_contact_sum
  - 0.5 * cumulative_squared_action
```

This supplies one common numerical benchmark for the realised trajectories. It is not an aligned or true-performance objective.

### 4.3 Primary diagnostic

Net forward progress at the 300k target remains the primary diagnostic. The six checkpoint profiles are dependent secondary observations of behavioural development. No best checkpoint is selected.

## 5. Recomputed final-checkpoint evidence

The paired differences below are `ctrl_cost_weight=0.0625 - default comparator` after averaging ten evaluation episodes within each policy.

| Training seed role | Seed | Condition-objective return | Common-rescored return | Net progress | Cumulative squared action | Unhealthy termination rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Discovery | 20260808 | +153.37 | -1,552.11 | -7.02 | +3,562.12 | -0.60 |
| Fresh replication | 20260809 | +157.40 | -1,362.06 | -2.70 | +3,004.61 | -0.30 |
| Fresh replication | 20260810 | +434.03 | -996.96 | -2.34 | +2,961.53 | -0.70 |
| Three-seed descriptive mean | - | +248.26 | -1,303.71 | -4.02 | +3,176.08 | -0.53 |

The positive condition-objective difference partly follows mechanically from the smaller action penalty and must not be described as improvement under a fixed proxy. The common-rescore difference is negative in all three seeds, as are the net-progress differences, while unhealthy termination is lower. The evidence therefore indicates a trade-off: policies trained with reduced control cost survive longer and terminate less often, but use substantially larger squared actions and achieve less net forward progress under the recorded evaluation protocol.

## 6. Hypothesis status

- **H1, control-cost misspecification:** supported only in a narrowed exploratory sense. Reduced-cost policies show lower progress and lower common-rescored return, but better unhealthy-termination, drift and orientation summaries. Uniformly worse locomotion is not supported.
- **H2, monotonic amplification:** not supported. Temporal results may be described as late-emerging and non-monotonic.
- **H3, proposal-conformant mitigation:** not tested. Forward reweighting is a separate exploratory intervention and displays possible failure displacement.
- **H4, scalar-return sufficiency:** the measurement record is consistent with the principle that one scalar is insufficient to characterise the observed multi-objective trade-off.

## 7. Statistical boundary

Training seed is the unit of independent replication. The ten episodes within each policy do not increase the training-seed sample size. With three paired seeds, the minimum exact two-sided sign-test p-value for a unanimous direction is `0.25`. Formal v1 is therefore descriptive; it does not support conventional significance or broad robustness claims.

## 8. Retrospective metric limitations

Formal v1 records cumulative squared actions, reward components, episode termination flags, progress, lateral offsets and torso-angle summaries. It does not retain torso-height or qpos trajectories. Low-z collapse, high-z excursion and non-finite termination cannot be recovered retrospectively. Existing `fall` values must be relabelled `unhealthy termination` without inventing a subcategory.

The legacy effort-per-distance ratio is undefined for non-positive progress and unstable near zero. Formal-v1 reports must show cumulative squared action, progress and the undefined proportion rather than reporting only finite ratios.

## 9. Approved conclusion

> At the 300k target, policies trained with `ctrl_cost_weight=0.0625` had numerically larger condition-specific objective returns than policies trained with the default `0.5` comparator, but the reward definitions differed. When the recorded trajectories were rescored under a common control-cost weight of `0.5`, all three paired differences favoured the default-comparator policies. Net forward progress was also lower in all three reduced-cost policies, whereas unhealthy termination and several orientation diagnostics improved. The results therefore support an exploratory, simulation-specific multi-objective trade-off rather than uniformly worse locomotion or formal reward hacking.

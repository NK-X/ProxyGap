# Smoothness mechanism pilot protocol

Status: frozen authorised development protocol; formal launch prohibited.

## 1. Problem statement

The preceding hybrid-guardrail development matrix reduced some extreme posture
failures but did not produce consistently direct, low-impact locomotion. A fixed
replay of one visibly problematic endpoint policy showed repeated take-offs,
substantial no-floor-contact time and large raw contact-force peaks. A broader
one-seed replay of all 18 endpoint policies indicated that the behaviour was not
confined to one selected video. These observations are development evidence, not
a formal prevalence estimate.

## 2. Construct separation

This pilot keeps three concepts separate:

1. **Policy-output smoothness**: consecutive proposed actions change gradually.
2. **Body-level smooth locomotion**: limited flight time, vertical velocity,
   angular motion and impact while retaining the commanded forward motion.
3. **Specified biological gait**: a declared contact-phase pattern such as crawl,
   trot, pace or bound.

The pilot addresses the first two concepts. It does not claim to learn a natural
or reptile-like gait because no gait family or reference phase pattern has been
declared.

## 3. Mechanistic rationale

Gymnasium Ant-v5 rewards forward velocity linearly, adds a fixed healthy reward,
and subtracts control and clipped contact costs (Farama Foundation, 2026). The
formula has no direct action-rate or gait-phase term. It can therefore reward a
brief high-speed take-off even when the movement is visually abrupt.

Raffin, Kober and Stulp (2022) report that unstructured step-wise exploration in
deep reinforcement learning can produce jerky robotic motion and propose smooth
state-dependent exploration. Aractingi et al. (2023) use bounded command-velocity
tracking and first- and second-order action-difference penalties in quadruped
locomotion. These sources support testing the mechanisms; they do not establish
that the present coefficient values are optimal.

## 4. Frozen intervention

The pilot is a two-by-two factorial screen:

| Condition | Forward objective | Action-rate weight |
|---|---|---:|
| Flinear__Ar0 | linear forward velocity | 0.0 |
| Ftrack__Ar0 | bounded target-velocity tracking | 0.0 |
| Flinear__Ar0p2 | linear forward velocity | 0.2 |
| Ftrack__Ar0p2 | bounded target-velocity tracking | 0.2 |

The tracking reward is

\[
r_{\mathrm{track},t}=\exp\left[-\left(
\frac{v_{x,t}-1.0}{0.5}\right)^2\right].
\]

The normalised action-rate penalty is

\[
p_{\Delta a,t}=\frac{\lVert a_t-a_{t-1}\rVert_2^2}{4d},
\qquad d=8,
\]

and contributes \(-0.2p_{\Delta a,t}\) when enabled. The first transition has
zero penalty. Both expressions are bounded to prevent a rare extreme value from
dominating the complete objective.

All conditions retain orientation cosine shaping at 0.1, lateral-velocity shaping
at 0.05, default control cost 0.5, the same PPO configuration and the same 113
dimensional observation. The external action-slew projector is disabled so that
the measured smoothness is generated by the policy rather than imposed after the
policy acts.

## 5. Replication and evaluation

- Training seeds: 41401, 41402 and 41403.
- Evaluation seeds: 51401-51410, paired across every condition.
- Training budget: 300,000 timesteps per condition and seed.
- Checkpoints: 100,000, 200,000 and 300,000 timesteps.
- Endpoint replication unit: independently trained policy, not evaluation episode.
- Contact replay: evaluation seed 51401 for every endpoint policy.

Primary diagnostics remain disaggregated: fixed-horizon forward velocity,
proposed and applied action roughness, no-floor-contact fraction, take-off count,
raw contact-force peaks, path efficiency, direction error, torso tilt and unhealthy
termination. Complete videos are qualitative evidence only.

## 6. Interpretation gate

The action-rate mechanism is supported only if it reduces proposed action
roughness in the same direction across development seeds without unacceptable
loss of commanded motion. The target-tracking mechanism is supported only if it
reduces flight or impact diagnostics while retaining velocity near the command.
If the combination remains hopping-dominant, the next bounded question may test a
body-level vertical-motion or foot-contact term. It must not be declared successful
merely because a video looks better.

No coefficient in this pilot may be retuned from the resulting outcomes. No
formal mitigation, natural-gait, general Ant-v5 or real-robot conclusion is
permitted.

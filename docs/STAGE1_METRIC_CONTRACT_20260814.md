# Stage-One Metric Contract

**Status:** frozen for the 2026-08-14 development grid; subject to user review
before held-out formal training.

## Task and reward

`Ant-v5` has no fixed destination. Over at most 1,000 environment steps, the
agent receives reward for positive-x translational velocity and for remaining
healthy, with penalties for action magnitude and contact force. At the default
time increment `dt = 0.05 s`, a full episode represents 50 simulated seconds.

For coefficient `w`, the unshaped condition objective is

\[
R_w(\tau)=\sum_{t=1}^{T}
\left(r_t^{\mathrm{forward}}+r_t^{\mathrm{survive}}
+r_t^{\mathrm{contact}}-w\lVert a_t\rVert_2^2\right).
\]

The per-step forward term is

\[
r_t^{\mathrm{forward}}=\frac{x_{t+1}-x_t}{\Delta t}.
\]

It is translational velocity, not gait cadence. The survival term is the
environment's implicit duration reward: a healthy Ant receives `+1` per step.

## Matched proxy comparison

For every candidate coefficient `w`, candidate and reference trajectories are
both scored under `R_w`:

\[
\Delta R_w = \bar R_w(\pi_w)-\bar R_w(\pi_{0.5}).
\]

This removes the invalid comparison between raw returns generated by different
reward formulae. It does not make `R_w` a true-performance measure.

## Behavioural diagnostics

Net forward progress is

\[
D_x=x_T-x_0,
\]

and mean forward velocity is

\[
\bar v_x=\frac{x_T-x_0}{T\Delta t}.
\]

Because unhealthy policies may terminate early, velocity must be interpreted
together with episode duration and unhealthy-termination rate.

Forward path efficiency is

\[
E_{\mathrm{path}}=
\frac{x_T-x_0}{\sum_{t=1}^{T}
\sqrt{(x_t-x_{t-1})^2+(y_t-y_{t-1})^2}}.
\]

Action saturation is an action-bound occupancy diagnostic, not measured motor
saturation:

\[
S=\frac{1}{8T}\sum_{t=1}^{T}\sum_{j=1}^{8}
\mathbb{1}(|a_{t,j}|\ge 0.95).
\]

Normalised command roughness is

\[
Q=\frac{1}{32(T-1)}\sum_{t=2}^{T}
\lVert a_t-a_{t-1}\rVert_2^2.
\]

The denominator is `4 x 8 = 32` because an action component in `[-1,1]` has a
maximum squared one-step change of 4. Therefore `Q` lies in `[0,1]`.

The environment calls a state healthy when all state values are finite and the
torso height lies in `[0.2,1.0]`. Unhealthy terminations are separated into
low-z collapse, high-z excursion, non-finite state and other unhealthy cases.
Lateral drift, torso tilt and command quality are broader diagnostics; they are
not folded into Gymnasium's narrow health definition.

Complete fixed-seed videos may reveal an inverted or otherwise implausible pose
while torso height remains inside the health interval. Such a video is evidence
that the constructs differ, not permission to replace the quantitative posture
metric with visual judgement.

## Independent and nested units

A training seed produces one trained policy and is the experimental replication
unit. Evaluation seeds generate paired initial perturbations after the policy is
frozen. Evaluation episodes are nested measurements used to estimate each
policy's behaviour; they are not additional independent training runs.

## Decision margins and limitations

The development margins are specified in
`configs/stage1_dense_development_v1_20260814.json`. They prevent numerical noise
from being labelled as behavioural harm. They are project decision thresholds,
not externally validated safety limits. All candidate decisions must be repeated
at half and twice the nominal margins before any held-out protocol is frozen.
